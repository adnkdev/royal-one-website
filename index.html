About Royal One

Delivering affordable energy efficiency improvements for Victorian homes and businesses.

Royal One is a VEU (Victorian Energy Upgrades) Accredited Provider committed to helping Victorians reduce their energy bills and greenhouse gas emissions. We participate in government-backed energy efficiency programs to deliver sustainable solutions that are more affordable for consumers and reduce long-term energy consumption.

With over 5,000+ Victorian residents trusting us for their energy needs, we have established ourselves as a leading provider of premium air conditioning, heat pump, solar, and battery storage solutions. Our mission is to make energy efficiency accessible and affordable for every household and business across Victoria.

Today, Royal One continues this commitment by providing comprehensive home heating and cooling systems, solar panels, and battery storage solutions. We help Victorians embrace sustainable energy independence while significantly reducing their utility costs and environmental impact.
