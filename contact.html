Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about contacting Royal One and our services.

How can I contact Royal One?

You can reach us by phone at (03) 9000 0000, email at info@royalone.com.au, or by filling out the contact form above. We're available Monday to Saturday, 8:00am to 6:00pm.

What services does Royal One offer?

We specialize in air conditioning systems, heat pump hot water, solar panel installations, and battery storage solutions for residential and commercial properties across Victoria.

How long does it take to get a quote?

We typically provide quotes within 24-48 hours of receiving your enquiry. For complex projects, it may take up to 3-5 business days to complete a full assessment.

Do you offer emergency services?

Yes, we provide emergency repair services for existing installations. Contact us immediately if you have an urgent issue, and we'll prioritize your request.

Are your services covered by warranty?

Yes, all our installations come with comprehensive warranties. Equipment warranties range from 5-10 years depending on the system, plus our workmanship guarantee.

Do you work with commercial properties?

Absolutely! We serve both residential and commercial clients. Our team has extensive experience with commercial energy solutions and can handle projects of any size.
